How It Works

Up and running in under a minute

No training required. If you can text, you can use Flat-Rate Tracker.

Install to home screen

Open the app in your phone browser and tap "Add to Home Screen." Done — no App Store, no account until you're ready.

Log your first job

Enter hours worked and what you did. Attach a photo of the RO if you have one. Tap Save. That's the whole flow.

Review OCR suggestions

The app reads stock numbers and VINs from your photo. Tap Apply to pull a suggestion into the record — it never writes without you.

Compare your pay stub

When you get paid, enter your stub total on the More page. See immediately if the numbers match what you logged.
