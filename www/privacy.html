Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 24, 2026

Flatrate Buddy ("the App") is built for automotive flat-rate technicians to log jobs, hours, and earnings. This policy explains what data we collect, how it's used, and how it's protected.

1. What We Collect

The App collects only the information you enter directly:

  • Employee number (stored locally and used as an identifier for cloud sync)
  • Job entries: work type, hours, RO/STK number, earnings, VIN, and date
  • Optional job photos you attach
  • App preferences (hourly rate, color theme, weekly goal)

We do not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers, or any other personal information unless you explicitly sign in.

2. How Data Is Stored

Locally (no account required): All data is stored in your device's browser storage (IndexedDB / localStorage). It never leaves your device unless you choose to sign in.

Cloud (optional, sign-in required): If you sign in, your job entries and photos are synced to a private Supabase database hosted on AWS. Your data is stored under your account and is not accessible to other users.

3. How Data Is Used

Your data is used solely to display your logged jobs, calculate earnings summaries, and generate reports you request. We do not:

  • Sell, share, or trade your data with any third party
  • Use your data for advertising
  • Run analytics or tracking on your activity
  • Send unsolicited communications

4. Photos

If you attach a photo to a job entry and are signed in, the photo is uploaded to secure private cloud storage (Supabase Storage). Photos are accessible only through a short-lived signed URL generated on demand for your account. Photos are never indexed, shared, or analyzed.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses Supabase for optional cloud backup and authentication. Supabase processes data in accordance with their privacy policy. No other third-party services are used. The App contains no advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or social media integrations.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Local data persists until you clear your browser/app storage or uninstall the app. Cloud data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete individual entries or your entire account at any time from within the App.

7. Security

All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Cloud data is stored in Supabase with row-level security policies ensuring each user can only access their own records.

8. Children

The App is intended for working automotive technicians (adults). We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
