Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Built specifically for flat-rate automotive technicians. Not a generic time tracker — a tool that speaks your language.

🔧
Log Jobs Instantly
Tap job type, enter flat hours, hit save. Your most-used jobs appear as one-tap chips. The whole thing takes under 5 seconds.
💰
Catch Short Pay
Enter your check amount after payday. The app compares it against every logged job and flags any gap automatically.
📊
Track Your Week
Daily bar chart, weekly goal ring, effective $/hr, comeback rate — every number that tells you how your week actually went.
📷
Scan Repair Orders
Point the camera at an RO or Get Ready sheet. The app reads the RO number, VIN, and job list automatically.
⏱️
Clock In / Out
Track your shift and see your efficiency ratio — flat hours earned vs. time on the clock. Know when you're ahead or behind.
☁️
Cloud Backup
Sign in to sync your full history to the cloud. Switch phones or reinstall — every job you ever logged comes back instantly.
