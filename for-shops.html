Common questions

Before you set it up

Does this cost the shop anything?
No. Setting up a shop and adding techs is free. Individual techs can optionally pay for their own Pro features (dispute PDFs, exports, pay-stub scanning) — that's between them and the app, not billed to the shop.
Does it plug into our DMS or payroll system?
No integration required. Techs log their own hours on their own phone; the Team Dashboard just gives you a shared view of that and any disputes they raise. Nothing to install on your systems.
Can I see everything a tech logs?
The Team Dashboard shows your roster and any claims a tech chooses to raise — it's not a live feed of every job they log. It's built to surface disputes, not monitor every entry.
What if I need to remove someone or hand off manager access?
You can remove a member from the roster at any time. Handing off manager access to someone else is on the roadmap — for now, reach out and we'll help directly.
